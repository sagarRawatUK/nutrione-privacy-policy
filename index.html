Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 28, 2026

Welcome to NutriOne. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the NutriOne mobile application ("App").

By using NutriOne, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Name (if provided)
  • Email address (for account creation and sign-in)
  • Date of birth
  • Height and weight
  • Fitness and nutrition goals
  • Referral code (optional)

Nutrition and Food Data

When using NutriOne, you may provide:

  • Photos of meals for AI food recognition
  • Food logs
  • Calorie and macronutrient entries
  • Weight history
  • Exercise entries

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash logs
  • Diagnostic information

2. Camera Access

NutriOne requests camera permission so you can scan meals using AI food recognition. Photos are only used to analyze food and improve your nutrition tracking experience.

You can deny camera permission and continue using manual food entry features.

3. Health Information

If you choose to connect Apple Health or Android Health Connect, NutriOne may access health-related information such as:

  • Weight
  • Steps
  • Workouts
  • Active calories

Health data is accessed only after your explicit permission and is used solely to provide requested features.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide AI food recognition
  • Calculate calorie and macronutrient goals
  • Track nutrition progress
  • Display personalized insights
  • Sync your data across devices
  • Improve app performance
  • Provide customer support
  • Manage subscriptions

5. AI Food Recognition

When you scan food, images may be processed using AI technology to identify food items and estimate nutritional values. These estimates are intended for informational purposes and may not always be perfectly accurate.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with trusted service providers necessary to operate the App, including:

  • Authentication providers (Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In)
  • Subscription management (RevenueCat)
  • Cloud storage providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Crash reporting services

These providers process data only as needed to provide their services.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures including:

  • Encrypted communication (HTTPS)
  • Secure authentication
  • Protected cloud storage
  • Access controls

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we strive to protect your information.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information while your account remains active or as needed to provide our services.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account
  • Export your data
  • Withdraw permissions

10. Children's Privacy

NutriOne is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that such information has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

11. Third-Party Services

NutriOne may integrate with third-party services, including:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Apple Sign-In
  • Apple Health
  • Health Connect
  • RevenueCat
  • Google Play Billing
  • Firebase (Analytics and Crash Reporting)

The privacy practices of these providers are governed by their own privacy policies.

12. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed or stored in countries outside your own. We take reasonable measures to protect your information during such transfers.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes become effective when posted on this page. The updated effective date will always appear at the top.
