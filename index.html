1. Who is responsible

NutriOne is a nutrition and calorie tracking app with AI meal recognition. It is developed and published by Sagar Singh, an individual developer based in India, under the developer name “Myx”. NutriOne is not published by a company.

In this policy, “I”, “me” and “my” mean Sagar Singh. I decide how the information described here is used and I am responsible for it. Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 I am the “data fiduciary”, and under the GDPR I am the “data controller”. I am also the grievance contact for NutriOne.

2. Information I collect

Account

  • Your email address and an account ID.
  • Your name, if you provide it or share it through Sign in with Apple or Google. With Apple you can hide your real email address.
  • Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are never visible to me.

Profile and health information you enter

  • Date of birth, gender, height, current, starting and target weight, activity level, weight goal, nutrition targets and daily water goal.
  • Your onboarding answers, preferred units and any referral code.

Tracking data

  • Foods and meals you log, with calories and nutrients, saved foods and custom meals, recipes you save or build, exercise sessions and weight entries.
  • Water intake, body measurements and fasting sessions.
  • Meal plans and shopping lists.

Usage and device information

  • App events such as “food logged” or “scan completed”, with non-identifying details such as meal type. They never include food names, photos, your name or your email.
  • Crash and error reports with technical details such as device model, OS version and app state.
  • The number of AI scans you make each day, the extra scans you earn by watching ads, and the number of AI coach messages you send each day, used to apply plan limits.

Advertising (free plan only)

  • Google AdMob collects device and ad information to show ads, as described in Advertising.

3. Camera, photos and AI logging

NutriOne uses the camera to scan meals and barcodes. When you scan a meal, the photo is sent to my server and to the AI provider to estimate its nutrition, and it is not kept on my servers after the estimate is returned. A copy stays on your device next to your log entry and is deleted when you log out or delete your account.

You can also type or dictate a description of a meal, such as “2 eggs and toast”. The text is sent to my server and to the AI provider in the same way and is not kept on my servers. Dictation uses your phone’s keyboard; NutriOne does not record audio.

For barcodes, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts. When you search for a food, only the words you type are sent to Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central. You can deny camera access and log food manually.

4. AI coach

Premium includes an AI nutrition coach. When you ask it something, your question and the last few messages of the conversation are sent to my server and to the AI provider, together with your goal, your targets and what you have logged that day, so the answer can use your numbers. Your name and email are not included.

The coach’s replies are not stored on my servers. Your conversation is saved only on your phone. My server keeps a count of how many messages you send each day to apply the daily limit. The coach gives general nutrition guidance, not medical advice.

5. How I use information

  • to calculate calorie and macro targets, store your logs and show progress, reports and insights;
  • to sync your data across devices;
  • to estimate the nutrition of meal photos and descriptions, look up barcodes and search for foods;
  • to answer your questions through the AI coach;
  • to send reminders you turn on, which are scheduled on your device;
  • to unlock Premium and keep your subscription in sync across devices;
  • to show ads on the free plan, and to confirm with Google that you finished a rewarded ad before giving you an extra scan;
  • to keep the service secure, prevent abuse and enforce plan limits;
  • to understand usage and fix problems.

I do not sell your personal information. I never use your health information, such as your weight, measurements, food logs or photos, for advertising or for anything other than providing NutriOne.

6. Advertising (free plan)

On the free plan, NutriOne shows ads from Google AdMob: banners, ads between screens after you log several meals, and optional rewarded ads you can watch for an extra AI scan. Ads do not load during onboarding or sign-in, and Premium subscribers see no ads.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising identifier (if you allow it), your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. When you watch a rewarded ad, Google also receives your NutriOne account ID so my server can verify the reward. Google never receives your name, your email, your health information, your food logs or your photos.

Where the law requires it, NutriOne asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before any ad loads. On iPhone you may also see Apple’s tracking prompt; if you decline, Apple’s advertising identifier is not shared. Google uses this data under its own advertising policies.

To limit this

  • Change your choice in Profile → Ad Privacy Choices (shown where the law requires it).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, or turn off tracking for NutriOne in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Upgrade to Premium to remove ads.

7. Subscriptions

Premium is purchased through the App Store or Google Play and managed by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your NutriOne account ID and the store’s purchase and subscription records, so Premium can be unlocked on all your devices. It never receives your name, email or health information. I receive only whether your subscription is active, and I never see your card details.

8. Legal bases

India: your consent when you create an account and enter information, and the legitimate uses allowed by the DPDP Act, 2023.

EEA, UK and Switzerland:

  • Contract: to provide the features you use, including your account, sync, AI logging, the AI coach and Premium.
  • Explicit consent: for health information such as weight, body measurements and food intake.
  • Consent: for personalised ads and for the ad provider to store or read identifiers on your device.
  • Legitimate interests: security, preventing abuse, understanding usage and fixing crashes.

You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the information or your account, or, for ads, in Profile → Ad Privacy Choices.

9. Service providers

  • Supabase: hosting, database, authentication and server functions.
  • Anthropic: AI analysis of meal photos and descriptions, and the AI coach’s answers, sent without your name or email. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not train on this data and may keep it for a limited period for abuse monitoring.
  • RevenueCat: subscription management.
  • Sentry: crash and error reporting, configured not to collect your name, email or IP-based identity.
  • Open Food Facts: barcode lookups and food search (the barcode number or search words only).
  • USDA FoodData Central: food search (search words only).
  • Google: AdMob advertising on the free plan, Google Sign-In, Google Play distribution and purchases.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, App Store distribution and purchases.

Supabase, Anthropic, RevenueCat and Sentry act on my instructions under data processing terms. Google uses ad data under its own terms.

I may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of users.

10. How long data is kept

  • Your account and tracking data are kept while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, see Deleting your account.
  • Usage events, daily scan and coach counts and rewarded-ad records are deleted with your account. Crash reports do not identify you and are deleted automatically by Sentry within 90 days.
  • Meal photos, meal descriptions and coach conversations are not stored on my servers.
  • Ad data is kept by Google under its own retention policies.

11. Deleting your account

You can delete your NutriOne account and its data at any time, in the app or without it.

In the app

  1. Open Profile, then Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Without the app

Contact me from the email address on your account and ask me to delete it. I will confirm the account is yours, delete it within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • your account and profile, including date of birth, gender, height, weights, goals and targets;
  • your food logs, saved foods, custom meals, recipes, exercise sessions, weight entries, water logs, body measurements, fasting sessions, meal plans and shopping lists;
  • your usage events, daily scan and coach counts and rewarded-ad records;
  • the meal photos, coach conversation and data stored on that phone.

What is kept

  • Copies in the hosting provider’s encrypted backups, until they are overwritten a short time later.
  • Crash reports, which do not identify you and are deleted by Sentry within 90 days.
  • Purchase records, which Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat keep as tax law requires. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your store account.
  • Ad data already collected by Google, under its own retention policies.

12. Your rights and choices

Edit your details in Profile → Personal Details, Goals and Nutrition Targets, export your data in Profile → Settings (Export as CSV or Export Data), and delete your account and data in Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Reminders, camera and notification permissions can be turned off at any time, and you can limit ads as described above.

California residents: I do not sell personal information. On the free plan, Google may receive your device’s advertising identifier and IP address, which may count as “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can opt out in Profile → Ad Privacy Choices or by limiting ad tracking in your device settings. I do not use sensitive personal information beyond what is needed to provide NutriOne.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to:

  • access the personal data I hold about you and get a copy in a portable format;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to (India).

To make a request, contact me. I will reply within 30 days and may need to confirm that the data is yours first. If you are not satisfied with my answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India or, in the EEA or UK, to your local data protection authority.

13. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, your sign-in session is kept in your device’s secure storage, the database restricts each account to its own data, and AI and subscription keys are kept on the server, not in the app. No system is completely secure, but if a breach affects your data I will notify you and the relevant authorities where the law requires.

14. Children

NutriOne is not intended for anyone under 13 (or the higher minimum age where you live). I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through NutriOne, contact me and I will delete it.

Where data leaves the EEA or UK, the transfer relies on safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses in the provider’s terms.
